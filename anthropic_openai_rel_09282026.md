## 🔍 Key Themes This Week
- **Power and permitting are the bottleneck, not demand.** Oracle's force majeure on Stargate NM and CNBC's report that Anthropic and OpenAI are chasing smaller 20–30 MW sites to get capacity online faster both point to the same constraint. This favors near-term capacity holders (neoclouds, Akamai-style CPU inference) and grid/power names (GEV, PWR, VST), and adds risk to gigawatt-scale projects that depend on new builds.
- **Anthropic keeps diversifying compute.** Recent deals include Akamai ($11.6B, CPU inference), Nscale ($45B, Vera Rubin, WV), Volta ($10B, Norway) and the Google/Broadcom TPU deal (3.5 GW from 2027). The spread across vendors supports NVDA, AVGO and GOOGL at once and weakens the "single cloud partner" framing for AMZN.
- **"Pace the frontier" overhang.** Amodei's Sept 12 essay, backed by Altman, Musk and Nadella, triggered a ~6% chip selloff. It targets frontier *training runs*, not data center buildout, and inference demand keeps growing. Headline risk remains, though, if it turns into policy.
- **Capital raising is picking up.** Corning ($2B ATM), CoreWeave ($3B convertible) and SoftBank ($11.1B high-yield for OpenAI) all show the buildout moving to external financing. Michael Burry's warning about capex write-offs in 2028–29 adds to the skeptical case.
- **Micron's print is the week's sentiment test** for the memory supercycle, which drives MU, LRCX and AMKR.
