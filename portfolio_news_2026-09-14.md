# Portfolio News Briefing — September 14, 2026

## 🔴 HIGH IMPACT

### 1. AI Slowdown Call Sends Tech Stocks Sliding — **Bearish** for AI infrastructure plays
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a lengthy essay calling for a materially slower pace of frontier AI development. OpenAI's Sam Altman, Microsoft's Satya Nadella, Google's Demis Hassabis, and Elon Musk all publicly endorsed a more deliberate, coordinated approach citing safety concerns. The statement rattled markets: Nasdaq futures fell 1.8%, ASML dropped 6% in Europe, and South Korea's KOSPI sank 3.3% on a 6.4% fall in SK Hynix. Premarket weakness visible across NVDA, AMD, INTC, ARM.

**Affected tickers:** NVDA, AMD, INTC, ASML, AVGO, ARM, MRVL, SOXL, KORU, EWY, SKHY

> **Why it matters:** If the six largest AI model labs slow inference and training compute scaling, demand for GPUs and datacenter buildout may plateau sooner than expected. This directly challenges the multi-year revenue ramp priced into chipmakers and neocloud providers.

---

### 2. Saudi Arabia East-West Pipeline Shut After Drone Attack — **Bearish (macro)**; **Bullish (energy)**
Drones struck Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline on September 12–13, forcing a shutdown of the 7 million bbl/day artery that normally bypasses the Strait of Hormuz. Brent crude surged to ~$108/barrel (+3.5% today, +9% WoW), with West Texas Intermediate approaching $103. Yanbu port holds only 5–7 days of export inventory per Reuters. US diesel prices crossed $6/gallon for the first time.

**Affected tickers (macro):** XLF, SPY, QQQ, TQQQ, SOXL, AAPL, AMZN, MSFT, TSLA, COST, NKE, ABNB

> **Why it matters:** Oil at $108 is an inflationary shock. Higher energy costs compress consumer discretionary, transportation, and logistics margins. Rate expectations may shift hawkish. Energy ETFs (XLE, not in portfolio) benefit; broad market faces headwind.

### 3. IREN — JP Morgan Double Upgrade to Overweight, PT $65 — **Bullish**
JP Morgan reversed from Underweight to Overweight on IREN (IREN Ltd.) and raised the price target to $65 from $46, citing IREN's emergence as a "top tier neocloud provider." Catalysts include: (1) 50-MW Microsoft facility deployed, 150-MW balance due by year-end 2026; (2) Strategic NVIDIA partnership; (3) ARR projection raised to $4.0B from $3.4B. This is a notable reversal from the same firm.

> **Directional: Bullish** — Major brokerage flip from UW to OW with a 41% PT increase is a high-conviction signal. Timing may be counterintuitive given the AI slowdown narrative but Microsoft contract execution de-risks near-term revenue.

### 4. SWKS — CEO Confirms Qorvo Merger in Final Stages, +9.1% — **Bullish**
Skyworks Solutions CEO Phil Brace stated at an investor conference that the company's $22 billion merger with Qorvo is in its final stages, with only two regulatory jurisdictions remaining for approval. SWKS reached a new 52-week high. The combined entity would be a dominant RFFE (radio frequency front-end) supplier.

> **Directional: Bullish** — Regulatory risk is the last overhang; CEO confirmation of near-completion is a deal-closing signal. Watch for antitrust conditions.

### 5. AVGO — Fiscal Q3 Blowout: AI Revenue +221% YoY, Guidance Raised — **Mixed**
Broadcom reported fiscal Q3 2026 results on September 4 (impacting the sector this week): revenue of $29.59B (+85.5% YoY), EPS of $3.32 non-GAAP (+96.4% YoY). AI semiconductor revenue hit $16.7B (+221% YoY). Raised FY2026 AI guidance to $58B and projected $115B for FY2027, $230B for FY2028. FCF record at $13.67B (46% of revenue). Dividend: $0.65, ex-date September 21, 2026.

> **Directional: Mixed** — Results are exceptional. Stock is down 14.6% over the past month amid broader AI selloff. Today's AI slowdown news adds further near-term pressure. Fundamentals remain strong; timing risk is the concern.
