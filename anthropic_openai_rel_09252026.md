## 🔍 Key Themes This Week
- **Demand narrative vs. deal flow:** The "pace the frontier" essay (Sep 12) spooked hardware and optics. Anthropic still signed Akamai ($11.6B) within two weeks of it, after Nscale (~$45B) in August. So far, deals contradict the demand-slowdown fear.
- **Execution and financing risk is rising:** Oracle's force majeure, Corning's ATM raise, and Akamai's $5.5B capex need all show the buildout is getting harder to finance and permit. Local and political opposition to data centers is growing ahead of the November midterms.
- **Smaller, faster sites:** Labs are pivoting toward 20–30 MW deployments. That favors modular power and cooling (VRT, ETN, NVT, MOD) and neocloud/edge providers (CRWV, AKAM) over mega-campus developers.
- **Macro headwind:** A hawkish Fed (25bp hike last week) and higher yields weigh on long-duration AI multiples. Highest risk for richly valued names (LITE, COHR, CRWV, OKLO).
- **China decoupling reaches networking:** The SASAC survey extends "domestic chips" pressure from GPUs to Ethernet switching (AVGO, potentially MRVL).
