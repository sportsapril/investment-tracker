# Portfolio News Briefing — September 1, 2026

> **Macro backdrop:** S&P 500 closed down 0.3% (7,686), Dow –0.7%, Nasdaq –0.1%. U.S. and Iran resumed active hostilities overnight — two oil tankers struck by projectiles in the Strait area. Crude oil and long-bond yields spiked. Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish weekend remarks pushed September rate-hike odds to ~57% (from 40%). September historically the weakest month for equities.

## 🔴 High Impact

### AAPL — Apple | **Mixed**
> John Ternus officially becomes Apple CEO today (Sept. 1), replacing Tim Cook who moves to Executive Chairman. Stock is down ~1.7% on the session as leadership transition draws scrutiny. BofA reiterated Buy with $380 target (~20% upside). First major test: iPhone launch event Sept. 9 featuring first-ever foldable iPhone. Apple dropped its net-cash-neutral target, signaling a shift toward higher R&D, AI acquisitions, and CapEx. Company is using Google's Gemini as the foundation for Siri, having struggled to develop its own AI models.
*Directional call: **Mixed** — CEO transition creates near-term uncertainty; Sept. 9 iPhone launch is a near-term catalyst. BofA bullish on AI opportunity.*

### NVDA — Nvidia | **Bullish**
> Fiscal Q2 results (reported Aug. 26) blew past expectations: revenue +106% YoY to $96.2B, non-GAAP EPS +120% to $2.22. Shares jumped ~9% the following session. Additional: NVDA announced a $3.5B investment in Taiwanese chipmaker MediaTek (adopting Nvidia tech for custom AI chips). Cash dividend of $0.25 declared, ex-date Sept. 10. Stock up ~16% YTD but lagging PHLX Semiconductor index (+62%).
*Directional call: **Bullish** — blockbuster earnings, strategic partnership, dividend affirm AI dominance.*

---

### TSLA — Tesla | **Bullish**
> Stock up ~4.9% today. Cybercab (fully autonomous robotaxi, no steering wheel) set for public launch in Austin, Texas on **Sept. 3**. Nevada Transportation Authority unanimously approved permits for Tesla, Waymo, and Uber to run commercial robotaxis in Clark County (up to 8,000 driverless vehicles over 12 months). Cybercab production has begun at Gigafactory Texas. FSD gained approval across five European countries.
*Directional call: **Bullish** — Cybercab launch imminent, regulatory tailwinds accelerating autonomous monetization.*

---

### MRVL — Marvell Technology | **Mixed**
> Record Q2 earnings (reported Aug. 27): revenue +37% YoY to $2.739B, EPS $0.94. Significantly raised FY27 and FY28 revenue outlooks. Craig-Hallum raised PT to $300 (from $217), keeps Buy. Despite the beat, stock fell ~8% to $222.25 — market appears to have priced in even stronger guidance. Down from recent highs.
*Directional call: **Mixed** — fundamentals excellent, sell-the-news reaction; PT upgrades suggest long-term upside.*

---

### AVGO — Broadcom | **Watch (Earnings Tomorrow)**
> Q3 FY2026 earnings report scheduled for **tomorrow, Sept. 2**, after market close. Consensus: $29.43B revenue (+85% YoY), EPS $2.55. AI semiconductor revenue target is $16B for the quarter. Context: after Q2 beat in June, shares dropped 12.6% because management didn't raise FY2027 AI revenue target above the "in excess of $100B" prior guidance. Risk/reward is asymmetric heading in.
*Directional call: **Watch** — beat is expected; real question is whether AI revenue guidance gets lifted beyond $100B FY2027.*

---

### GOOG — Alphabet | **Bearish (Near-term)**
> Q2 results beat on almost every line: revenue +24%, cloud +82%. But stock fell ~8% (~$330B market cap lost) as CapEx doubled to ~$45B and free cash flow turned **negative for the first time as a public company**. Wall Street spooked by capital intensity outrunning cash generation.
*Directional call: **Bearish near-term** — FCF turning negative is a structural concern; will need evidence that CapEx translates to durable revenue growth.*

---

### GLD / SLV — Gold & Silver ETFs | **Mixed**
> Gold fell below $4,450/oz today as hawkish Fed remarks dominated. Markets pricing ~57% probability of a September rate hike. However, U.S.-Iran conflict escalation (oil tankers struck overnight) is a tail risk supporting safe-haven demand. Silver forecast to hit $80+ by end of 2026 per J.P. Morgan (conservative) and up to $150 (bullish targets). Short-term: bearish on rate-hike pricing; medium-term: geopolitical tail risk supportive.
*Directional call: **Mixed** — rate-hike headwinds vs. Iran conflict safe-haven demand.*
