### GOOG — Alphabet Inc. | **Bearish**
Stock fell 2.1% today. Alphabet posted **negative free cash flow for the first time ever** in Q2 (-$5.85B), after raising 2026 capex guidance to $195–205B for AI infrastructure build-out. Bill Ackman's Pershing Square exited all GOOGL shares in Q2, switching to NFLX, citing negative FCF concerns. Shares remain ~2% below pre-Q2 earnings levels.
**Direction: Bearish** — first-ever negative FCF is a structural concern, and capital discipline scrutiny is rising across hyperscalers.
Sources: [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/07/23/news-google-raises-2026-capex-guidance-again-above-us200b-free-cash-flow-turns-negative-for-first-time/) | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/hyperscalers-face-higher-capex-scrutiny-after-alphabet-report-panned.html)
