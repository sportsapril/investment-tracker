## 🔴 HIGH IMPACT

### NVDA — Nvidia | **Bullish**
Nvidia Q2 FY2027 earnings report drops **Wednesday, August 26** (after market close). Analysts broadly expect a beat — the company has exceeded EPS estimates in each of the last four quarters. Management has disclosed an order book exceeding **$1 trillion** for 2026–2027. Shares are up ~18% from the July 29 low. BMO named NVDA a "Top AI Pick" on Aug 21 alongside AVGO, MRVL, MU, and AMD.
> *Directional: Bullish — outsized order book visibility + earnings track record; watch for any guidance revision on export restrictions.*

### BABA — Alibaba | **Bearish (near-term)**
Alibaba finalized a **HK$80 billion ($10.21 billion) secondary share placement** in Hong Kong at HK$112.70/share — the largest-ever primary follow-on offering by an HK-listed company. Shares fell **~8%** in Hong Kong Monday. The capital raise is earmarked for AI-related development, but the immediate dilution and a separate report of **75% net income drop** (alongside cloud growth) are weighing on sentiment.
> *Directional: Bearish near-term — dilution overhang and profitability concerns; bullish medium-term if AI cloud spend converts.*

---

### TSLA — Tesla | **Bullish**
Last Thursday, Nevada regulators unanimously approved Tesla for **up to 5,000 paid robotaxi permits** in Clark County (Las Vegas metro) — replacing a prior 10-vehicle cap. Commercial launch expected within ~30 days once administrative steps are complete. Stock surged 4–6% Friday. Waymo and Uber also received approvals (1,000 vehicles each). Separately, **Europe Semi** launch also contributed to Friday's rally.
> *Directional: Bullish — major regulatory unlock for revenue-generating robotaxi operations; competition from Waymo/Uber is real but Tesla's fleet size approval is 5x larger.*

### IREN — IREN Limited | **Bullish**

> *Directional: Bullish — contract delivery milestone + Nvidia partnership ahead of earnings; high EBITDA margins validate pricing power.*
