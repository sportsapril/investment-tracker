**AMD — Advanced Micro Devices** *(Tier 1: Chips & Silicon | Rating: BUY)*
- **Relevance:** Anthropic
- **Headline:** AMD commits up to $5B equity investment in Anthropic tied to a 2GW Instinct MI450 deployment deal.
- **What happened:** Announced July 22, AMD will invest up to $5B in Anthropic as part of an infrastructure partnership where Anthropic deploys up to 2GW of AMD Instinct MI450/MI455X GPUs (paired with EPYC "Venice" CPUs and Pensando networking in AMD's Helios rack architecture). - **Analyst notes:** Rosenblatt named AMD a "top pick," raising PT to $665 from $490; UBS raised PT to $700 (Buy) ahead of AMD's AI conference this week.
- **Price impact:** Bullish — direct, deepened Anthropic compute relationship plus bullish sell-side PT hikes.
