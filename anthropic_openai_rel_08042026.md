## 🔍 Key Themes This Week
Hyperscaler capex is diverging in market reception: Amazon and Microsoft were rewarded for their AI infrastructure spend and cloud growth (AWS/Azure), while Alphabet's guidance raise to $195–205B initially triggered a selloff before recovering — the market is shifting from "spend whatever it takes" to "show us the returns," with combined 2026 hyperscaler capex now tracking above $700B.

AMD is emerging as a credible second-source GPU supplier to frontier labs, following its Anthropic ($5B investment, 2GW MI450) and prior OpenAI/Meta commitments — total AMD AI compute commitments across the three labs now approach 14GW, a direct challenge to Nvidia's near-monopoly.

Nuclear and advanced-reactor power for AI data centers is accelerating as a policy priority, with the DOE/Trump administration fast-tracking Oklo and X-Energy alongside existing PPAs (Constellation-Meta) and SMR partnerships (BWXT-GE Vernova-Hitachi); Vertiv's new Oklo cooling tie-up shows infrastructure vendors increasingly co-designing around nuclear-adjacent sites.

Optical/photonics names (Lumentum, Coherent, Corning, Applied Optoelectronics) rallied sharply after Nvidia's Jensen Huang publicly reinforced the shift toward optical interconnects in AI racks, a bullish read-through for the Tier 6 networking cohort even as some of these names remain volatile week to week.

Packaging and OSAT names showed a split picture: Amkor posted record revenue and a new 10-year TSMC deal but sold off hard on cautious Q3 guidance, while Celestica beat estimates and rallied — a reminder that even inside the same infrastructure theme, execution and near-term guidance are driving dispersion.
