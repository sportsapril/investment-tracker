**Watchlist-wide — Anthropic vs. Pentagon** *(Cross-tier legal/policy catalyst | Anthropic-specific)*
- **Relevance:** Anthropic
- **Headline:** A federal judge ruled the Pentagon's "supply chain risk" designation of Anthropic unlawful (ruling issued Aug. 28, still reverberating in coverage this week).
- **What happened:** U.S. District Judge Rita Lin found Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk was "illegal and baseless," constituting unlawful First Amendment retaliation and a Fifth Amendment due-process violation tied to Anthropic's refusal to loosen safety guardrails around autonomous weapons and mass surveillance use cases. The Trump administration has appealed; a second Anthropic suit is pending before the D.C. appellate court. This removes a federal-contracting overhang that had let OpenAI take Pentagon business Anthropic was blocked from bidding on.
- **Analyst notes:** No direct rating changes, but removes a government-revenue risk specific to Anthropic's federal/defense channel (relevant to AWS/GOOGL cloud partners hosting Claude for government workloads).
- **Price impact:** Bullish for Anthropic's federal contracting prospects; indirectly positive for AMZN/GOOGL as its primary cloud/compute partners.
