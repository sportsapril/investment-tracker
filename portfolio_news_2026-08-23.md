## 🔴 HIGH IMPACT

### MRVL — Marvell Technology | **Bullish**
> Google AI chip deal gives Marvell warrants to buy $12.2B in shares; revenue potential ~$120B through FY2033.

Announced August 19, Marvell disclosed an expanded custom AI chip agreement with Alphabet/Google. Google received warrants to purchase up to ~59M shares at $206.58/share (exercisable through 2033), with additional shares vesting per $500M in custom product revenue. Marvell will develop AI inference accelerators, storage, networking, and memory interface controllers for Google. Stock jumped +12% on the news. This closes the custom silicon "hyperscaler sweep" for Marvell, which now has deals with all major cloud providers.

**Why bullish:** Landmark revenue visibility ($120B potential), hyperscaler lock-in, fills the Google gap that rivals couldn't.

### AVGO — Broadcom | **Mixed**
> Fell ~5% on MRVL-Google deal, recovering premarket Aug 23; separate $60B AI financing deal in talks.

Broadcom dropped intraday after the Marvell-Google custom chip announcement, as investors feared AVGO's own Google custom chip business could face competition. Shares stabilized Aug 22-23 as analysts noted Broadcom still holds substantial custom silicon relationships. Separately, Broadcom is reportedly in talks to raise $60B+ in debt for an AI chip financing structure benefiting AI labs including Anthropic. AVGO earnings scheduled **September 2**.

**Why mixed:** Near-term competitive concern from MRVL-Google, but Broadcom's own hyperscaler relationships and massive earnings catalyst ahead argue for patience.
