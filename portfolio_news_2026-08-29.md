## 🔴 High Impact

### NVDA — Nvidia | **Bullish**
**Blowout Q2 earnings + Hugging Face acquisition reported**

Nvidia reported fiscal Q2 2027 revenue of **$96.2B** (vs. $92.27B consensus) and non-GAAP EPS of **$2.22** (vs. $2.09 expected). Q3 guidance came in at **$108B**, well above expectations. Shares surged **+8.7% Thursday** on the print, adding over $400B in market cap.

Separately, Nvidia reportedly agreed to acquire **Hugging Face for $12.9B** (CNBC, citing The Information). The deal is not yet signed and could still fall apart. Hugging Face is the central open-source AI model hub with ~$150M in revenue — the acquisition would extend Nvidia's reach into software/model ecosystem beyond chips. Data Center order book stands at $1 trillion for 2026–2027 vs. $253B trailing twelve-month revenue.

*Rationale: Earnings beat + massive guidance + strategic M&A all point strongly bullish; only risk is deal uncertainty on Hugging Face.*

### MRVL — Marvell Technology | **Mixed**
**Earnings beat, stock fell ~8% on valuation**

Marvell reported Q2 FY2027 revenue of **$2.74B** (beat consensus by 1.2%) and non-GAAP EPS of **$0.94** (beat by 1.19%). Data Center revenue grew **+46% YoY**. Management raised FY27 revenue guidance to ~**$11.5B** (+40% YoY) and FY28 to ~$16.5B. Despite the beat, shares fell approximately **8%** on high valuation concerns and mixed investor reaction to a new Google custom silicon deal.

### BABA — Alibaba Group | **Bearish**
**Dilutive HKD 80B placement + multiple securities fraud lawsuits**

Alibaba priced a **HKD 80B (~$10.3B) new share placement** at HKD 112.70/share (3.6% discount to US close) to fund AI infrastructure investment. Shares fell **~9% in the Hong Kong session**. Separately, multiple law firms (Rosen, Hagens Berman, Levi & Korsinsky, SBS Law) filed securities fraud class action lawsuits against Alibaba, citing allegedly false/misleading statements between June 2025 and June 2026. BABA declined from $173.68 high (Oct 2025) to $95.07 (June 2026) — a 45% drop during the class period. Lead plaintiff deadline is **October 5, 2026**.

*Rationale: Dilution + legal overhang + ongoing share price recovery from a deep drawdown are all headwinds.*
